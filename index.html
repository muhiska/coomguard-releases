  1. Open the downloaded file and allow the install. Android warns that the app is not from the store; that is expected.
  2. Android 13 and newer: Settings, Apps, CoomGuard, the ⋮ menu, Allow restricted settings.
  3. Open CoomGuard, tap Enable and turn on CoomGuard under Accessibility.
  4. Updates: the app checks for newer builds on its own.
